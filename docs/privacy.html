Privacy Policy: GTA 6 fun clips GAZ

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos created by the app to your YouTube channel;
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel name, so that you can check which channel the videos go to.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the computer where the app runs. It is not sent to any server other than Google's. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data with third parties and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services, see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, delete the local token file and the app can no longer access your account.
